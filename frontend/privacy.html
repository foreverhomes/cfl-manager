CFL Manager

Privacy

Last updated October 16, 2026

CFL Manager is a private tool for the general managers of the Competelevel Fantasy League, a fantasy hockey league. This page explains what it keeps about the people who sign in.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your email address and your name from Google. It stores those, the fantasy team you're linked to, your role, and when you last signed in. It doesn't ask Google for anything else: no contacts, files, calendar or other account data.

Why

Only to know who you are: to let the league's admin approve you, to link you to your team, and to decide what you can see and change in the app.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Signing out removes it. The app uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

League data

The rest of the app's data is about the league: rosters, standings and scores from Fantrax, player stats from NHL.com, and contracts from the league's salary sheets.

Where it's kept and who sees it

The data is stored in the app's database, hosted by Neon, and the app runs on Vercel. Your email and name are visible to the league's admin. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Removing your data

Ask the league's admin to remove your account. That deletes your email, name and team link from the app straight away.
